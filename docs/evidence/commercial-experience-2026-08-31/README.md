## Visitor job and hypothesis

The visitor must understand the decision, consequence, compressed work, artifact and limits of an offer; choose the smallest sufficient layer; understand the next state of every action; and receive a persisted CONFENGE receipt before any confirmation.

The hypothesis is that one consistent experience reduces uncertainty and improves useful progression toward a qualified commercial opportunity. It does not claim causal uplift, ROI, ranking, demand, urgency or client result.

## 100-repetition test

Repetition improves the system: the next offer inherits the versioned ladder, renderer, form profile, receipt rules and gates. It does not require an independent page-specific credit sentence or a manual CTA spreadsheet.

## Rollback

Revert the integration commits by SHA. This restores the prior generated routes and source contracts without changing URLs, stored leads, external data authority, Warmbly state or protected measurement windows.
