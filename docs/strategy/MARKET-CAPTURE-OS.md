## Corporate thesis

CONFENGE is a B2G intelligence company applied to engineering that monetizes that intelligence through high-value services. Consulting is the current monetization mechanism, not the only compounding asset.

The system is designed for increasing returns:

`data → intelligence → opportunity → acquisition → conversation → client → delivery → learning → reusable data/method/proof/content/product → better acquisition and delivery`.

The supreme question for work is: after doing this 100 times, are we merely busier, or is the system materially better?

## North Star

**Qualified Commercial Opportunities Created per Week.**

A QCO requires a valid account, specific problem/opportunity, fit with a CONFENGE offer, traceable evidence, temporal reason and plausible decision unit/route. Raw leads, messages, commits, PRs and closed issues do not count.

Subordinate outcomes: qualified conversations, meetings, proposals, weighted pipeline, won revenue and expansion revenue. UNKNOWN remains UNKNOWN; WON requires human/documentary confirmation.

## Five-front roadmap

1. **REVENUE NOW:** Decision Unit, 1–3 validated routes, operator queue, founder-led canary, conversations and proposals.
2. **INBOUND ENGINE:** confenge.com.br, first public vertical, tools, SEO, conversion and flagship validation.
3. **MARKET INTELLIGENCE MOAT:** canonical entities/contracts/events, provenance, commercial intent, public-read richness and benchmarks.
4. **COMPOUNDING SYSTEM:** outcomes, knowledge extraction, institutional proof, reusable delivery and land-and-expand.
5. **SCALE / SUNSET:** automate only proven processes; simplify infrastructure; migrate and archive SmartLic.

## Operating records

Commercial selection is event-led, not company-list-led:

`account × contract × event × problem/opportunity × offer × decision unit × route × timing`.

Every trigger has evidence/provenance. The truth plane may structure who/why/why now/offer/decision unit/route, but it does not become CRM or messaging. Warmbly records approved action and observed outcomes. Delivery closeout evaluates confidentially safe reusable derivatives and the next client action.

## Leverage and decision states

Work should create at least one of revenue, distribution, data, automation, trust or customer leverage. Use `EXECUTE_NOW`, `VALIDATE`, `DEFER`, `SUNSET` or `SUPERSEDED`; sunk cost is not priority.

Expected strategic value weighs revenue, acquisition, LTV, defensibility, compounding and cost reduction against effort, complexity and time to evidence.

## Decisions of market capture

### Codified

- One CONFENGE brand/domain; SmartLic is legacy only.
- Founder-led discovery precedes automation/volume.
- Dominant ICP hypothesis: engineering/construction businesses with material public portfolios and under-proportioned internal B2G capability.
- Work from evidenced triggers toward concrete offers and a decision unit/route.
- Initial wedges: contract economic protection; portfolio/opportunity and GO/NO-GO intelligence; executive B2G diagnosis.
- Public intelligence is a product-quality acquisition asset, not a content landfill.
- Client won is not terminal; delivery/outcome must feed reuse, proof and next action.
- QCO/week is the North Star; pipeline/outcomes cannot be inferred.

### Pending validation

- Final operational ICP thresholds and exclusion segments.
- Winning trigger × offer × decision-unit × route combination.
- Entry-diagnosis content, unit economics, conversion and next offer.
- Flagship asset's narrow first wedge and eventual name.
- Partner proposition/incentive and first repeatable channel.
- Land-and-expand minimal record and renewal cadence.
- Retainer/module/value-component pricing mix.
- Consent-safe institutional proof format and thresholds.

### Contradictions removed

- SmartLic as product/public inbound brand.
- Company lists without a specific event/problem/offer/timing.
- Bulk or auto-send before a winning commercial combination.
- Raw lead/message/development volume as success.
- Generic engineering/enrichment without a near-term consumer.
- Client won as terminal and delivery as one-off labor only.

### Elevate when evidence arrives

- Promote the winning offer/ICP combination into a commercial ADR.
- Promote the flagship public product thesis into a product ADR after prototype evidence.
- Promote market-learning/outcome schema after the first real canary.
- Promote pricing or partner models only after observed commercial evidence.

## STOP DOING

- Stop all SmartLic product, brand, automatic deploy, acquisition and independent runtime work.
- Freeze bulk outreach, auto-send and volume expansion until a real trigger/ICP/offer/route combination converts.
- Defer broad enrichment, generic adapters and platform engineering without an identified P0/P1 consumer or truth blocker.
- Stop measuring success by content/page count, raw leads/messages or development throughput.
- Do not build a large CRM, partner portal, sophisticated forecast or inferred WON/expansion.
- Do not automate consulting processes before repetition, rules, exceptions and observable errors are proven.
- Do not create many offers, observatories or brands; validate a few buyable wedges and one flagship asset.
- Do not preserve priority because code or backlog already exists.
