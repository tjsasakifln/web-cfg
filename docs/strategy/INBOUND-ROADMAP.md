## North star

Make `confenge.com.br` Brazil's most useful, cited and trusted public acquisition
surface for companies that bid for and execute public works. The North Star is
**Inbound Qualified Pipeline per Month**. Category success means CONFENGE is
widely known in AEC B2G and respected for verifiable technical authority;
commercial success still requires attributable pipeline and revenue.

## Benchmark principles

- Answer a real task first; optimize search discovery second.
- Show source, method, coverage and freshness close to every public claim.
- Start with an executive overview, then allow filtering/exploration and details on demand.
- Make reusable datasets, charts and citations easy to download, link and embed.
- Expand programmatically only when each page has distinct utility and quality gates.
- Measure impressions, qualified clicks, engagement, assisted conversions and pipeline by surface.

## Sequence

- **Now:** first margin-defense vertical, conversion/attribution contract, safe inbound handoff and critical Search Console instrumentation.
- **Next:** contract/price explorer, one flagship research edition, entity/editorial authority and migration map execution.
- **Then:** profiles, observatory, indices, radar and tender hub after reuse, differentiation and quality gates are proven.

## Quality gate

A new indexable surface must have an identified audience/job, unique utility, canonical URL, provenance, freshness state, empty/error states, internal links, no unsupported claims, useful CTA, analytics without PII and an accountable owner. Failing the gate means no indexing or expansion.

## Scorecard

- Search: non-brand impressions, clicks, qualified query coverage and index/crawl health.
- Product: useful interactions, return rate, exports/embeds and freshness/SLA.
- Commercial: qualified leads, attribution completeness, next-action creation, assisted pipeline and revenue.
- Authority/distribution: branded search, qualified citations, relevant referring domains, artifact reuse and direct/returning demand.
- Efficiency: reusable components/contracts, editorial review load and cost per maintained surface.
