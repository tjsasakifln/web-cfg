# Source compatibility (`CONFENGE_COMPOUNDING_STANDARD/1.0`)

Machine matrix: [`data/growth-accounting/source-compatibility-matrix.json`](../../../data/growth-accounting/source-compatibility-matrix.json).

Incompatible or partial sources are consumed as UNKNOWN/BLOCKED overlays. They are never zero-filled and never mixed into the non-branded primary series.

SmartLic historical demand is `legacy_brand` (anti-baseline). It cannot authorize a CONFENGE compounding or exponential state.
